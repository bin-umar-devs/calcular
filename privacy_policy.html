Privacy Policy for SimpleCalc

Last updated: September 1, 2026

SimpleCalc ("the App") is a basic calculator application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

SimpleCalc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, calculation data, or usage data. The App performs all calculations locally on your device. No data ever leaves your phone.

Permissions

SimpleCalc does not request any special device permissions (such as access to your camera, contacts, location, storage, or internet). The App has no network access requirements to function.

Third-Party Services

SimpleCalc does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no third-party SDKs embedded in the App that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

SimpleCalc does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because the App collects no information at all.

Data Security

Because no data is collected or transmitted by the App, there is no user data at risk of being accessed, lost, or misused through the App's operation.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted at this same location along with an updated "Last updated" date.
